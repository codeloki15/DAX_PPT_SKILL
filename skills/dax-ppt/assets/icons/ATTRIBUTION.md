# Icon attribution

`icons.json` catalogues every file in this folder.

- `data-axle-logo.svg` is the Data Axle wordmark, used with the brand's colours.
- The 512x512 PNG icons were supplied from the maintainer's own downloads. Their
  naming matches the Flaticon catalogue; Flaticon's free licence requires visible
  attribution to the author and to flaticon.com wherever the icons are used.
  Add the specific author credits here before distributing decks externally, or
  replace the set with icons under a licence that suits your use.
